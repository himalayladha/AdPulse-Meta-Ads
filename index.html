Connecting to the Meta Ads API

Follow these simple steps to hook this dashboard up to your live Meta advertising records:

  1. 1
    Create a Developer Application: Go to the Meta for Developers Console, register an account, and click Create App. Choose "Other" or "Business" and complete the registration.
  2. 2
    Add Facebook Login & Marketing API: In your application dashboard, add the Facebook Login product and the Marketing API product.
  3. 3
    Configure Redirect URIs: Under Facebook Login Settings, add your development domain (e.g., http://localhost:3000 or your hosting domain) to the "Valid OAuth Redirect URIs".
  4. 4
    Copy App ID & Log In: Copy your App ID from the top menu, paste it into the API Keys card to the right, and authenticate your Facebook account to fetch active campaigns!
Developer Sandbox Note: In development mode, only administrative accounts, developer profiles, or authorized test users inside your Meta developer dashboard can log in and retrieve reporting records.
