Meta App Setup Wizard

Step 1 of 5

Step 1: Register as a Meta Developer

To communicate with Meta APIs, you need to sign up as a developer on their official platform.

  1. Go to developers.facebook.com.
  2. Click Log In in the top right using your standard Facebook account.
  3. Click Get Started and accept the Meta Developer Agreements.
  4. Confirm your email and complete the developer questionnaire.

Step 2: Create a Business Application

You must register a new application under your developer profile to receive an App ID:

  1. Go to your App Dashboard and click **Create App** (green button).
  2. Under App Use Cases, choose **Other** or **Business**, and click **Next**.
  3. Select **Business** as the App Type. This is critical for portfolio/campaign integrations.
  4. Set a Display Name (e.g. `My Reporting Tool`) and choose your Business Portfolio, then click **Create App**.

Step 3: Add Login and Marketing API

Add the mandatory authentication and reporting products to your new App ID:

  1. In the left-side panel of your Meta App Dashboard, find **Facebook Login** and click **Set Up**.
  2. Scroll down and locate the **Marketing API** product card, then click **Set Up**. This links your App to Meta's insights endpoints.

Step 4: Configure Redirect URIs

Authorise this dashboard's domain name so Facebook can securely log you in:

  1. Expand **Facebook Login** in the sidebar -> click **Settings**.
  2. Under **Client OAuth Settings**, locate the **Valid OAuth Redirect URIs** input field.
  3. Copy and paste your active domain origin (shown below):
http://localhost:3000

Also, ensure **Login with JavaScript SDK** is toggled to **Yes** and add the same URL to **Allowed Domains for the JavaScript SDK**.

Step 5: Activate Access & Connect

Facebook Apps start in Development Mode, restricting access to testers:

  1. **Sandbox Check**: In the sidebar, go to **App Roles -> Roles -> Testers**. Click **Add Testers** and invite other users. They must accept the invitation on developers.facebook.com.
  2. **Go Live**: Go to **App Review -> Permissions & Features**, request Advanced Access for `ads_read` and `business_management`, and toggle the switch in the top bar to **Live**.
  3. Paste your new **App ID** into the visible input field at the top of the login card and click **Login with Facebook**!

Meta Developer Setup Guide

AdPulse executes API queries completely on the client-side. To connect your campaigns, you must register a Meta Developer Application in your Facebook Developer Dashboard. Follow our interactive checklist below:

1 Register as a Meta Developer

To integrate your app with Facebook services, you must register as a Meta Developer:

  1. Navigate to developers.facebook.com.
  2. Click Get Started or Log In in the top right-hand corner.
  3. Log in with your standard Facebook credentials.
  4. Follow the registration onboarding wizard: agree to terms, verify your mobile number, and select your developer profile as Marketer or Developer.
2 Create a Meta Application

Every API integration requires a unique Application ID (App ID):

  1. Inside your Meta App Dashboard, click Create App (green button).
  2. Select App Use Cases: Choose Other or Business (choose Other for maximum flexibility). Click Next.
  3. Select App Type: Choose Business (CRITICAL) as this unlocks Business Portfolios and the Marketing API. Click Next.
  4. App Details: Enter a display name (e.g. AdPulse Analytics), confirm your contact email, and select your Business Manager account (optional).
  5. Click Create App and enter your password to confirm.
3 Add Login & Marketing API Products

Your newly created app needs credentials to connect to Facebook's OAuth and Marketing endpoints:

  1. Scroll down the App Dashboard to the **Add products to your app** section.
  2. Find **Facebook Login** (or *Facebook Login for Business*) and click **Set Up**.
  3. Find the **Marketing API** product card and click **Set Up** (CRITICAL). This initializes SDK endpoints for campaign analytics under your App ID.
4 Configure OAuth Redirect URIs & SDK

To ensure secure logins, you must authorize this domain to receive Facebook's security tokens:

  1. In the left-hand menu, expand **Facebook Login** and click **Settings**.
  2. Scroll to **Client OAuth Settings** and find **Valid OAuth Redirect URIs**.
  3. Copy the URL below and paste it in that field, then hit Enter:
    4. http://localhost:3000
  4. Ensure the toggle **Login with JavaScript SDK** is set to YES.
  5. Add the same URL into the **Allowed Domains for the JavaScript SDK** list.
  6. Click **Save Changes** at the bottom.
5 Configure App Review & Go Live

While your app is in **Development Mode**, only accounts explicitly listed as project Testers can connect.

🚀 Sandbox/Developer Testing Quick Shortcut:

To test quickly, click **App Roles > Roles** in your sidebar. Click **Add Testers**, and add the test Facebook profile username. Crucial: The tester must accept the invitation on developers.facebook.com notifications to authorize!

To launch the app for all external users publicly:

  1. Paste the compliance assets from the left panel (**Privacy Policy URL**, **Terms of Service URL**, **App Domain**) into the fields under **Settings > Basic**.
  2. Go to **App Review > Permissions and Features**, locate `ads_read` and `business_management`, request **Advanced Access** (requires standard form explanation).
  3. Finally, toggle your App Mode from Development to Live at the top of your dashboard!
